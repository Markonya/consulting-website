Questions about the audit

Development project audit: questions and answers

Scope, documents, timing and engagement terms.

What does a development project audit include?

A review of the development schedule and critical path, the budget and CAPEX, contracts and tenders, the risk register and IRR/NPV sensitivity. You get an 8–12 page report, a 30-day action plan and a 60-minute review with management.

When does a developer or investor need an independent audit?

When you need to verify the numbers produced by the project team, understand schedule and budget variances, or assess a project before acquiring or financing it. An audit also helps when the contractor, general contractor or financial model changes.

Which documents are needed and how long does the express audit take?

To start, we need the schedule, the budget, the main contracts and the latest management report. The express audit takes 7 working days from the moment the documents are received and includes interviews with one or two project leaders.

How much does an investment and construction project audit cost?

The express audit starts at RUB 250,000. The fixed fee is confirmed according to the scope of the project before work starts. The price of a standalone consultation and of regular ongoing support is agreed individually.

Can I order an audit for a project in another region?

Yes. Marian Krotov is based in St Petersburg and works remotely across Russia. Work is carried out under NDA and documents are never passed to third parties. You can discuss the project on Telegram or by email.
