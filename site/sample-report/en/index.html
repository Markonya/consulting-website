06 · Sources and limitations

What supports the findings

In a real report, each variance is linked to a document, version date and data owner. Confirmed facts, the team’s forecast and the auditor’s assessment are kept distinct. Findings without supporting evidence are marked as preliminary.

  • Programme: baseline, progress, remaining durations and work-front constraints.
  • CAPEX: approved budget, actuals, commitments and cost to complete.
  • Contracts: agreements, changes, payments, claims and remaining commitments.
  • Economics: financial model and scenarios with key assumptions.
  • Risks: owners, mitigations, review dates and escalation triggers.

This sample demonstrates the reasoning format and report structure. In a real audit, findings depend on the materials provided, interviews and agreed scope. The initial conversation takes 30 minutes and requires no documents.
